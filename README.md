## What Is the Baile Charme AI Trend?

Charme is Rio's slow, synchronized dance to American R&B and soul. It grew out of the Black Rio soul parties of the 1970s. DJ Corello is credited with naming the slower set "charme" in the early 1980s, and since the early 1990s it has filled the street under the Madureira viaduct on Saturday nights. In 2019 Rio de Janeiro state declared the charme movement intangible cultural heritage.

In 2026, charme clips from Rio street parties spread across TikTok. Then an AI video of a white-haired grandpa took over, captioned **"ninguém botou fé no vovô"** — nobody believed in grandpa, and he out-danced the whole floor.

Baile Charme AI (baile charme IA in Portuguese and Spanish) puts you, your vovô or your friends in the middle of that floor: one photo, your own clothes, an AI-made Rio night street party.

## FAQ

### What is baile charme?

Baile charme is a charme dance party from Rio de Janeiro: slow, synchronized steps to R&B and soul. The best-known one has filled the street under the Madureira viaduct on Saturday nights since the early 1990s. Funk carioca is a different genre — some people search "Brazilian funk dance AI" but mean this same charme trend.

### What does "ninguém botou fé no vovô" mean?

Portuguese for "nobody believed in grandpa", the caption on the original AI video. The joke: the old man nobody expected anything from turns out to be the best dancer on the floor.

### Can I put two people, a group or a pet in the video?

Not at the moment. Each video uses one photo of one person.

### Will the video look like me?

The AI keeps your face, hair and outfit. Clear, front-facing, evenly lit photos work best; waist-up or full body with arms in frame.

### Is there a CapCut template? What's the difference?

Yes. The CapCut template runs inside the CapCut app and ends with a CapCut outro. Baile Charme AI works in any browser, keeps your outfit, lets you pick 10 or 15 seconds, adds no watermark and returns credits if a render fails.

### What happens if the video comes out wrong?

A failed render returns the credits automatically. If it finishes but doesn't look like you, try a clearer photo or contact support from your account.

### What happens to my photo?

It is deleted 24 hours after the video finishes or fails. See the [Privacy Policy](https://bailecharmeai.com/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Service](https://bailecharmeai.com/terms-of-service).
